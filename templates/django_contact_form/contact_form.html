Frequently Asked Questions

  • Comic buying and selling:
    We do NOT buy, sell, or trade comics. We are a database of information ABOUT comics only. We suggest you find either a local comic shop or an online retailer.
  • Advertising on the Grand Comics Database TM website:
    Due to policies with the United States Internal Revenue Service, we are NO LONGER accepting any advertising on our website.
  • Posting blog articles on the Grand Comics Database TM website:
    We do NOT publish any type of blog and therefore have no need for articles. If you are inquiring about posting blog articles, we will not reply to you.
  • Data use policies and technical questions:
    Our data is available for anyone to use, even commercially, at no cost, providing the user links back and attributes the data to the GCD from any end use. More information can be found in our extended FAQ. To have technical exchange with other users, please join the tech e-mail list at https://groups.google.com/g/gcd-tech.
  • Cloudfare messages when visiting our site:
    Unfortunately, we are often hit with a large amount of bot traffic, which impacts our legitimate users. To prevent this, we are using Cloudflare, an intermediary that helps to stabilize our web servers. Recommendations one can find for people who have issues with Cloudflare can be found in our extended FAQ.

To send an e-mail to us, please enter your name, email, and message and press submit.
