Mark characters on cover

Cover sequence of {{ story_revision.issue }}

Mark where the characters of the cover sequence are drawn on the cover.

  • Choose the character with "Mark".
  • Turn the cover with the slider until the character stands upright.
  • Draw a rectangle around the character: drag over the cover, or touch two opposite corners. With the keyboard, the arrow keys move the rectangle and Shift with the arrow keys changes its size.
  • After saving, each marked character shows the image cut out of the cover.
  • Mark a character only where it is clearly seen: the character page shows up to three of the marked images, picked at random.
  • "Clear" takes the rectangle off the chosen character.
  • "Return To Sequence" saves the marks and goes back to the sequence, for example to add a missing character; "Cancel" goes back without saving.
